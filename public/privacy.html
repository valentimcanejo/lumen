Privacy Policy

Lumen — Last updated: June 14, 2026

Lumen ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile learning application. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Account information (optional)

  • If you sign in with Google: your name and email address provided by Google.
  • If you sign in with Apple: your name and email address (or the relay address Apple provides).
  • If you use the app without signing in: a randomly generated anonymous user ID is created automatically. No personal information is collected.

Learning progress

  • Lessons completed, quiz scores, streaks, and daily session history.
  • Selected track, preferred language, and daily-time goal set during onboarding.

Device information

  • Push notification token (only if you grant notification permission).
  • General device locale used to serve content in your language.

Subscription information (Premium users)

  • Purchase and subscription status (active, expired, plan type), managed via RevenueCat and your Google Play or App Store account. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, microphone, or camera data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To sync your learning progress across devices when you are signed in.
  • To send optional push reminders for your daily study goal.
  • To personalise the content language and track selection.
  • To improve the app based on aggregated, anonymous usage patterns.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

Lumen uses the following services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, cloud database (Firestore), and file storage. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Sign-In — optional sign-in method (iOS). apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Expo / EAS — app delivery and over-the-air updates. expo.dev/privacy
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription purchases and entitlements for Premium. Receives purchase/transaction data from Google Play and App Store; does not have access to your payment card details. revenuecat.com/privacy

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. Anonymous accounts that have been inactive for more than 12 months may be deleted. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

5. Children's Privacy

Lumen is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. disable notifications in device settings).

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
